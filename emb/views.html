LEED v4/v4.1 Quality Views: 75% of the regularly occupied floor needs a direct line of sight to the outdoors through vision glazing, and 75% needs views with at least two of four kinds: (1) lines of sight in directions at least 90° apart; (2) at least two of nature or sky, movement, and objects at least 7.5 m beyond the glass; (3) a window within three times its head height; (4) a view factor of 3 or more. Sky and distance are worked out from the geometry; nature and movement come from the two switches.
