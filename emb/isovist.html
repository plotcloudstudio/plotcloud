Walls and furniture taller than the eye block everything behind them. Lower ones let the eye see over them, but hide what is just behind: a strip that ends where the sightline over the top comes down to the height you look for. The lower the eye, the longer the strip.

5Walk a path

Draw a route with the Path tool, then walk it. The observer walks at the eye height above, and every patch of floor they see adds up as they go: deep orange where it was in view longest and closest, pale yellow where it was only glimpsed from afar, gray where it was never seen. Each glance counts by how large the floor looks: 3 m away counts half, 6 m a fifth.
