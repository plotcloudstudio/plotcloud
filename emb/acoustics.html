Method & assumptions

Sabine: T60 = 0.161 V / Σ(Sᵢ αᵢ). One closed convex extruded room; no diffraction, transmission, scattering, air loss or furniture absorption. The imported reference model is never a semantic acoustic mesh.

Image sources: maximum order 3; reflection energy factors multiply (1−α). Visibility is checked against finite surfaces. The 3D sphere is clipped to the room and to valid reflection regions.

SPL: source SPL at 1 m + 10 log₁₀[1/r² + 16π/R], R=A/(1−A/S). Early estimates use valid image arrivals up to 50 ms. Late integrated energy: (16π/R) exp(−13.8155 × 0.05 / T60).

The decay chart is the normalized Sabine statistical decay, −60t/T60 dB. It is not the sparse order-3 impulse response.
