1Floor plan

Or drop it on the view. Accepts .3dm (Rhino), .ifc, .obj, .stl, .glb, .dae (SketchUp export), .dxf, or a PNG/JPG scan.

Doors must be open gaps. 3D models are read from the floor up to 2 m and drawings are cut at 1.2 m, so anything standing in a doorway reads as a wall and the ants cannot pass.
  • 3D models (Rhino, IFC, OBJ, STL, glTF, SketchUp): cut a real opening through the wall and leave the doorway empty. Lintels and ceilings above 2 m are ignored, so an empty doorway is open. Door leaves on objects or layers named door (and IFC doors) are left out automatically; anything else in the opening blocks it.
  • DXF: draw the door swing and leaf on a door layer, or set that layer to Door (open) below. Don't draw a line across the opening on a wall layer.
  • Scans: door leaves and swing arcs drawn in dark ink read as walls. Use Open a wall to paint them away.

3Analysis

Ants walk between every two places through the walkable floor, keeping about 0.25 m from walls and furniture. Short routes collect more pheromone, so round by round the walks gather into corridors. Each ant draws one thin line behind it as it walks. Where many ants pass, the lines pile up and darken: the network is what their walks leave behind. Lines from the first, exploring rounds are pink; the last rounds, which have settled on the best corridors, are deep purple.

Detail

Ant walks are worked out in seconds at any detail. Finer levels run more rounds and more ants, and draw more walks (up to 3,000), so the corridors come out sharper.
