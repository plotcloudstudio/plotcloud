    3Members & checks

    Checks: steel stress ÷ φFy and column buckling to CSA S16 13.3.1; glulam stress ÷ φ·fb; concrete columns Nf ≤ 0.4 f′c Ag as a first rule; beam deflection span/360 under live (snow on roofs) and span/240 total; storey drift h/500 under service wind (NBC 4.1.3.5). A preliminary sizing, not a code design: no connections, lateral-torsional buckling, vibration, fire or seismic.
