04Expression & export

Method & assumptions

2.5D exposed-surface raster. Overhangs, buried terrain, downpipes, sewers, groundwater, momentum and pressurised flow are not resolved. Buildings and roofs retain the top sampled mesh surface; semantic land-use recognition is not performed.

D8 follows the steepest descending neighbour. Priority-Flood supplies an acyclic order across equal-height flats; original elevations and depressions are retained for water storage. Accumulation is upslope area, not discharge.

NRCS event runoff: S = 25400/CN − 254 mm; Iₐ = λS; Q = 0 for P ≤ Iₐ, otherwise Q = (P − Iₐ)²/(P − Iₐ + S). Only incremental runoff enters the surface solver. Initial abstraction and retention losses are not subtracted twice.

Surface routing is a conservative, eight-neighbour storage-cell approximation using water-surface gradients and Manning velocities with adaptive time steps. Particles are explanatory, not calibrated water parcels. The event ends when rainfall stops; residual water may still be draining.

Sources: USACE / NRCS runoff · D8 flow direction · Priority-Flood.
