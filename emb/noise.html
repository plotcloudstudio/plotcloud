Each road's level is its average (LAeq, day 07–23 h) 10 m from the centre line, typical for its type and lanes in the map data. Type a measured or mapped level where you have one. Night is taken as 8 dB quieter, a typical drop.

3Ground

Soft ground takes a few dB off near the ground (ISO 9613-2, eq. 10); hard ground reflects.

4Analysis

Every road is a line of sound sources. Buildings shield what is behind them: sound bends over their roofs and round their ends (ISO 9613-2 screening, 500 Hz); soft ground and the air take a little more. Each façade of every storey is checked 1.5 m above its floor.
