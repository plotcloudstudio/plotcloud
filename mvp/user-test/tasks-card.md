# Tasks (one at a time, please)

You are doing a quick feasibility study for a site in downtown Vancouver. Think aloud as you go.

**T1.** Start a new project for **800 Robson Street, Vancouver**. Include the surroundings within **150 m**. Your plot is about
**40 × 30 m**, at the corner of the block nearest to the address marker.

**T2.** The moderator will point at one neighbouring building. In reality it is **45 m** tall. Make the model match.

**T3.** Draw a building of **6 storeys** that covers **about half** of your plot. What are its gross floor area and its FAR?

**T4.** How much of the **open part of your plot** gets **at least 2 hours of sun on 21 March**?

**T5.** Make a **second option** that is **twice as tall**. In one sentence: what do you gain and what do you lose compared with the
first option?

**T6.** Run the **full sun-hours simulation** on the option you have open, and **keep the result**.

**T7.** Close everything. Find your project again and open the **tall option**.
